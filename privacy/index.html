Privacy Policy

Effective: September 25, 2026

EO2MATE provides tools that help authorized businesses manage selling activities and customer interactions across supported channels, including Facebook Pages.

Information we process

Depending on the features used, EO2MATE may process account and business information, connected Facebook Page information, Page-scoped identifiers, comments and selling commands, order and checkout information, message-delivery information, and operational logs needed to provide and secure the service.

How information is used

We use information to connect authorized Pages, process selling activity, create and manage orders, deliver transaction and checkout information, provide customer support, prevent duplicate or unauthorized activity, maintain audit records, and secure EO2MATE.

Meta/Facebook data

EO2MATE accesses Meta/Facebook data only through permissions granted by the Page owner or authorized user and uses that data to provide the requested EO2MATE functions. Access can be revoked through Meta/Facebook settings or by disconnecting the Page from EO2MATE.

Sharing and retention

EO2MATE does not sell customer data. Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate the platform, subject to applicable safeguards. Data is retained only as needed for service operation, security, audit, legal, and legitimate business requirements.

Security

EO2MATE uses access controls and other technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect account, Page, order, and operational data.

Your choices and deletion

You may revoke connected-platform permissions and request deletion of data associated with your EO2MATE use. See the User Data Deletion Instructions.

Contact

For privacy, access, correction, or deletion questions, contact EO2MATE using the support/contact information associated with the EO2MATE service.
