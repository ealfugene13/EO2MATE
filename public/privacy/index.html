LEGAL · DATA & PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective October 11, 2026 · Applies to EO2MATE websites and services

This policy explains how EO2MATE handles information when business users connect a Facebook Page, manage selling workflows, or when buyers interact with a seller using EO2MATE.

In briefEO2MATE processes information to run the features a seller enables. Sellers control their customer relationships and order fulfilment. We do not sell personal information.

1. Who this policy covers

“EO2MATE,” “we,” or “us” refers to the EO2MATE service. This policy covers visitors to eo2mate.com, business owners and staff who use the EO2MATE dashboard, and buyers whose information is submitted to a seller through an EO2MATE-powered workflow.

For business account, access, and platform-security information, EO2MATE determines how that information is used to operate the service. When EO2MATE processes a seller’s buyer, comment, order, or fulfilment information on that seller’s instructions, the seller is responsible for its customer-facing purposes and notices, and EO2MATE processes the information to provide the service.

2. Information we process

  • Account and security details: login email, account and client identifiers, authentication/session information, multi-factor authentication status, and security or access events.
  • Business and Page details: business/client settings, authorized staff activity, Facebook Page name and identifiers, granted permissions, and connection status. OAuth access credentials are used to provide the authorized integration.
  • Social selling activity: supported post details, comment text and Page-scoped identifiers, timestamps, auction or sale activity, and records needed to match a buyer action to an order.
  • Orders and fulfilment: buyer name or platform identifier, items and quantities, order and payment status, shipping recipient name, phone number and address, delivery selections, and related transaction references.
  • Payment workflow data: checkout identifiers, payment method or status, amount, and provider references. Online checkout is handled by the payment provider. EO2MATE does not ask buyers to send card numbers or security codes through EO2MATE messages.
  • Support and technical information: messages you send us, service diagnostics, request logs, timestamps, and information needed to investigate errors, protect accounts, and maintain the service.

3. How information is collected

Information is provided by account holders, authorized Page administrators, staff, and buyers; received from Meta when an authorized user connects a Page; generated as the service processes orders and activity; or received from providers used for authentication, hosting, payment, or delivery features. EO2MATE’s browser session may persist authentication state so a signed-in user can continue using the dashboard.

4. Why we use information

  • Create and secure accounts, authenticate users, and enforce access controls.
  • Connect authorized Facebook Pages and process supported comments, posts, selling activity, and messages requested by the seller.
  • Create and manage orders, provide checkout and shipping workflows, and show payment or fulfilment status.
  • Provide support, diagnose failures, prevent duplicate or unauthorized actions, and maintain service reliability.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations, protect rights, and investigate suspected fraud, abuse, or security incidents.

We use information for these service purposes and do not use Meta data for unrelated advertising or sell personal information.

5. Facebook and Meta information

EO2MATE accesses Meta/Facebook information through permissions granted by the account holder or an authorized user. We use that information to provide the connected features requested by the seller, protect the integration, and maintain related records. Meta operates its own services under its own privacy terms. You can remove or limit the connection in your Meta account’s Business Integrations settings and disconnect the Page in EO2MATE where that option is available.

Revoking access stops future authorized access but may not remove information already stored for orders, audit, or security purposes. To request deletion of eligible stored information, follow the Data Deletion Instructions.

6. When information is shared

We share information only as needed to provide or protect EO2MATE:

  • The seller and its authorized staff can access buyer, order, and fulfilment information for the seller’s business.
  • Meta receives requests and information required to operate the connected Facebook integration.
  • Infrastructure and service providers process information for authentication, database and Edge Function hosting, static website hosting, payments, or delivery features. They receive only the information needed for their role and are subject to their own terms and applicable safeguards.
  • Authorities or other parties may receive information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect people, rights, or service security.

Payment and delivery options depend on what a seller enables. When a buyer moves to a provider-hosted checkout or delivery service, that provider’s privacy terms also apply.

7. Retention

We keep information while an account or feature is active and for as long as needed to operate the service, support the seller’s orders, maintain security and audit records, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations. When information is no longer needed, we delete or de-identify it where reasonably practicable. Backup copies may remain until they are overwritten through normal backup rotation, and limited records may be retained where a lawful reason applies.

8. Security

We use technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect information, including authenticated access controls and service-level security measures. No internet service or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Please protect your sign-in credentials and tell us promptly through the support channel available to your account if you suspect unauthorized access.

9. Your choices and rights

You may update account information, manage staff access, disconnect a Page, revoke Meta permissions, or request access, correction, objection, erasure, or blocking of personal information, subject to applicable law and the circumstances of the request. Buyers should also contact the seller who collected their order or shipping information, because the seller manages the transaction and fulfilment.

For deletion steps, see User Data Deletion Instructions. Requests may be limited where information must be retained for a lawful purpose, security, or an ongoing transaction or dispute. If you are in the Philippines, you may also contact the National Privacy Commission about your data privacy rights.

10. International processing and third-party services

EO2MATE relies on third-party providers to operate the service. Those providers may process or store information in locations different from where you live. Their handling is governed by their service terms and privacy notices, as well as applicable safeguards.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top shows when this version took effect. Material updates will be presented through the website or service when appropriate.

12. Privacy requests and contact

To make a privacy, access, correction, or deletion request, use the support/contact method provided by EO2MATE during account onboarding or in your account. State which account, business, Page, or transaction is involved and how you can be reached. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, or access tokens. Buyers may contact the seller/Page that handled their transaction for buyer or fulfilment records.
