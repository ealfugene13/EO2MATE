ACCOUNT CONTROL · DATA REQUESTS

User Data Deletion

Updated October 11, 2026

You can revoke EO2MATE’s connection to Meta and ask us to delete personal information associated with your EO2MATE account or connected Page. These are separate steps: revoking access stops future authorized access but does not automatically delete information already stored.

Are you a buyer?For an order, payment, or shipping record, contact the seller or Facebook Page that handled the transaction. You may also ask EO2MATE to delete eligible copies it processes for that seller.

Step 1: Revoke Meta/Facebook access

  1. Open your Facebook account’s Settings & privacy and find Business Integrations (Meta may change the menu name or location).
  2. Select EO2MATE and remove the integration or revoke the permissions you no longer want it to have.
  3. If you can still access EO2MATE, disconnect the Page from the Facebook connection area in your account.

Revoking access prevents EO2MATE from making future requests that require those permissions. It does not itself submit a data deletion request.

Step 2: Submit a deletion request

Contact EO2MATE using the support/contact method provided during account onboarding or shown in your EO2MATE account. Put “User Data Deletion Request” in the subject or first line and provide:

  • The email address used for your EO2MATE account, if you have one.
  • Your business or client name and the connected Facebook Page name or ID, if relevant.
  • For a buyer request, the seller/Page name and enough order information to locate the record, such as an order reference or approximate transaction date.
  • Which information you want deleted and a way for us to contact you about the request.

Do not include your password, one-time authentication code, Facebook access token, full payment-card details, or unnecessary sensitive information.

Step 3: Verification and processing

We may ask for reasonable information to verify your identity and authority over the account, Page, or request. We will review the request, delete or de-identify information that is eligible for deletion, and let you know if we need more information or cannot delete some records.

Some information may need to be kept for an active order, payment or delivery issue, security and fraud prevention, legal obligations, accounting, or the establishment or defence of a claim. If only part of a record must be retained, we will limit retained information to what is needed for that purpose where reasonably practicable.

What may be deleted

  • Connected Page and integration data that is no longer needed to provide an active service.
  • Account profile or access information that is no longer needed for security or legitimate records.
  • Eligible comment, buyer, order, or shipping information, subject to the seller’s instructions and any lawful retention need.

Information in backups may remain until it is overwritten through normal backup rotation. Records that must be retained will be restricted to the applicable purpose and deleted or de-identified when that purpose ends.

If you no longer have account access

You can still revoke EO2MATE in Meta Business Integrations. For the deletion request, use the EO2MATE support/contact method provided to you when the account was created. If you are a buyer, contact the seller/Page that collected your order information and ask them to forward any platform-data request that concerns EO2MATE.
