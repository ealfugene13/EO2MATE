User Data Deletion Instructions

Updated: September 25, 2026

You may request deletion of data associated with your use of EO2MATE and may separately revoke EO2MATE's access to connected Meta/Facebook assets.

1. Revoke Meta/Facebook access

Use your Meta/Facebook account's business integration or app settings to remove EO2MATE or revoke permissions you no longer want EO2MATE to have.

2. Request deletion from EO2MATE

Contact EO2MATE through its published support/contact channel and state that you are requesting deletion of your EO2MATE data. Include enough account or business information for EO2MATE to verify the request without sending passwords or access tokens.

3. Verification and processing

EO2MATE may verify that the requester is authorized for the affected account or business before deletion. After verification, EO2MATE will delete or de-identify data that is no longer required, subject to information that must be retained for security, fraud prevention, transaction records, legal obligations, dispute handling, or other legitimate requirements.

Connected Page data

Disconnecting or revoking a Facebook Page prevents future authorized access through that connection. A deletion request can also be used to request removal of eligible previously stored Page-related data.

Need help?

Use EO2MATE's published support/contact channel and reference “User Data Deletion” in your request.
